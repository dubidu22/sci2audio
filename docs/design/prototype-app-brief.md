# Surface: sci2audio app prototype (whole app)

Mode: Operate (reading column also serves Read). Scope: clickable high-fidelity prototype of the whole desktop app at 1280–1600 wide, light and dark, EN/DE.

Audience and job: students and researchers (and dyslexic, low-vision, ADHD readers) build a study library of papers: open, check what will be read, make the audiobook, listen with read-along, capture notes/quotes/cards/marks at the sentence, export to Zotero, Obsidian, Anki.

Flows: library (first run, empty, all paper states, open/paste inline) · paper Prepare (chapters, changes printed→read as→why with switches, language, voice, make) · making progress inline (pause, resume, cancel, failure keeps work) · Listen and capture · focus listening · Captures across library (by intent, review, export with connected/offline/queued) · Voices (packs, files, sizes, licences, consent) · Privacy.

Content: synthetic example papers only, labelled synthetic. No third-party texts, no invented user numbers.

Open: Zotero/Obsidian/Anki mechanism, voice notes, headphone/media-key capture (shortcut sheet only), the wordmark is a proposal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is your working copy of the paper. The voice sweeps the sentence it reads in highlighter yellow and every capture sticks out of the page edge as a coloured flag at its sentence. It refuses the category default: grey sidebar, blue highlight, a notes panel of rounded cards.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white bond paper on a cool grey desk ground, black ink, pencil-grey marginalia and struck-through cancels, a binder-cobalt rail as the shell. Highlighter fluorescents only with meaning: yellow being read, green note, blue quote to cite, pink review card, orange mark; never two in one region. Flags are small squared tabs. Atkinson Hyperlegible for the interface, Literata for reading, tabular figures for every number. Hairline rules, 4px corners, soft offset shadow only on the paper sheet.

STORY: The visitor sees their papers as a shelf with progress and flags, checks exactly what will be read and why, leaves while the audiobook is made, then listens and captures without losing the thread, and finally sends what they kept to their study tools.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Listen screen. Left: cobalt rail, 264px, wordmark, Library / Captures / Open / Voices, papers being read with progress and flag strip. Centre: the white paper sheet, 62–70ch, chapter and page crumb, the sentence being read swept yellow, struck citations in pencil, flags at the sheet's right edge. Right: one context panel, 340px (captures of this chapter, or changes, or outline). Bottom: full-width docked player, timeline with the same flags along its top edge, transport, time, speed, four capture keys. Primary action: play / capture.

FORM: The Working Reader (a student's marked-up course reader), my grounded candidate 3 of 7; seed:3238905d85655cae. Raises: one fixed size ramp for quantity (star atlas); dense thumbnail cells with persistent marks (convention catalog); chrome-free focus listening at poster scale (metro tiles); nothing disappears, it cancels (ticket wallet); one tabular figure style (datamatics). Signature interaction: the highlighter sweep advancing with speech, and the flag sliding out of the page edge on capture, mirrored on the timeline.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
