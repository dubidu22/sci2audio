2026-11-28 (#1231): two Roman numerals after a keyword joined by an em dash, spaced or not ("Kapitel I — II", "Chapters I—II"), are a range read whole as with an en dash ("Kapitel 1 bis 2", "Chapters 1 to 2"), never only the first numeral, but only where the dash reads as a range: the second numeral is larger than the first and is no lone I, V or X, which in prose is a pronoun or a letter after a parenthetical dash ("Chapter II — I argue", "Kapitel II — X ist hier klein" read "2 — I", "2 — X" as before); a falling pair ("Kapitel III — II") is left as printed whole (decided by Claude under Colin's go-ahead of 28 Nov 2026, #1229; Colin may overrule).
