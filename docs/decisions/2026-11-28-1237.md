2026-11-28 (#1237): a slash pair is a rate and stays as printed when its second word is an abbreviated time word ("words/min", "requests/sec", "Meter/Sek.") or when the pair stands right after a number ("3 dollars/user", "5 Euro/Nutzer": a quantity per something), so "20 men/women" and "In 2019 teachers/students" stay as printed too, the faithful reading of a slash that may mean "per" (decided by Claude under Colin's go-ahead of 28 Nov 2026, #1229; Colin may overrule).
