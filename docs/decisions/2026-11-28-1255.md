2026-11-28 (#1255, carrying out Colin's #622 ruling of 26 Nov 2026): a run of numeric citation brackets that mixes "and" and "or", or repeats "or", is one citation, which replaces the last clause of the #622 line in `docs/decisions.md` ("a run that mixes 'and' and 'or' is no list"): as an aside it goes whole ("It holds [1], [2] and [3] or [4] here." is "It holds here."), where the sentence needs it it reads once, a mixed run with each join word where it is printed and a comma elsewhere ("references 1, 2 and 3 or 4", "references 1 or 2, 3 and 4"), a run of "or" alone as an "and" run does ("[1] or [2] or [3]" reads "references 1, 2 or 3", as "[1] and [2] and [3]" reads "references 1, 2 and 3") (decided by Claude under Colin's go-ahead of 28 Nov 2026, #1229; Colin may overrule).
