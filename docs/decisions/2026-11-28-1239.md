2026-11-28 (#1239, #1254): two numeric citation brackets of one number joined by a dash ("–", "—", "-", the figure dash U+2012, the minus sign U+2212 or the hyphens U+2010 and U+2011) are one item, a range, as "[1–3]" is, also inside a longer run of brackets: "As shown in [1]–[3], [5]." reads "As shown in references 1 to 3 and 5.", "It holds [1], [3]–[5] here." is "It holds here.", and a run of two items joined by "and" or "or" is needed as a pair of two brackets is ("It holds [1]–[3] and [5] here." reads "It holds references 1 to 3 and 5 here.", as "[1–3] and [5]" does; "[1] or [3]–[5]" reads "references 1 or 3 to 5") (decided by Claude under Colin's go-ahead of 28 Nov 2026, #1229; Colin may overrule).
