# Effects of Noise Exposure on Sleep in Preschool Children

Several studies have examined the effects of environmental noise on young children (Smith et al., 2019; Jones & Lee, 2020). However, findings remain inconsistent [2–6, 16, 17]. Exposure to trauma may interfere with infants' normal develop- ment and cause diffi- culties in emotional pro- cessing.

We searched Medline/PubMed, PsycNet and ERIC from inception to March 11, 2015. Data are available at https://doi.org/10.1007/s10578-016-0626-7 and www.example.org/data. Contact: j.doe@uni-example.edu.

A total of n = 35 studies met the criteria (Fig. 1). Children aged 0–6 years were included, i.e., preschoolers and toddlers, e.g., in day-care. The mean age was M = 4.2 (SD = 1.1) years; the effect was significant (p < .05, 95% CI [1.2, 3.4]). Doses of 3.5 mg/kg were given at 37 °C. Approx. 1,234 participants (± 12%) were enrolled vs. 980 controls ≥ 18 years.
