## Pinned items

### PR A: lane:layout, non-critical (#457, #459, layout part of #458, part 2 of #456)

- **L1 (#458) wide band for body-size heads.** `Edges.band(box, page, wide=True)`: a line's middle in the top/bottom `edge_band` will do. Used only by `_apart` (for text lines; a graphic still stands wholly in the band, else a plot's axes would make its tick label a footer, `test_figure_labels_are_not_furniture`) and by `_body_heads`. Page numbers, small-line shape heads, footers and `_running_heads` keep the strict band (with the wide band everywhere, chi's table header "OPP-115" became a running head).
- **L2 (#458) a rule under the head goes with it.** In `_apart`, a drawing at most `line_width` em thick less than `apart` em from the edge lines before it is taken with them, so the white beyond the rule counts (`_Page._rule_under`). The rule is still no evidence of a head by itself: the repeat rules decide.
- **L3 (#458) page number inside the head line.** `_body_heads` compares the exact text (numbers kept, D-323) and also the text with a number at its start or end replaced by its offset from the page number (`_texts`: "bildungundnachhaltigkeit∙#54#" on every odd page). "Algorithm 1" on p3 and "Algorithm 2" on p5 stay text (offsets −2, −3); the coverage rule (`head_pages`, `head_share`) still applies.
- **L4 (#458) footnotes are never furniture.** `_Page._notes`: under a footnote rule (the thresholds of `footnotes._zones`: thin, lower half, `footnote_rule_width` em to `footnote_rule_share` of the text width), the lines starting within `footnote_reach` em of the rule's left end, set smaller than the body (`small`), that follow the rule and each other with less than `apart` em of white. Smaller letters alone no longer make them footers or running heads. A page number below them is still found (ACL p3: "354" right below the notes in the right column is body size, so it ends the stack).
- **L5 (#457) note number in the notes' own size.** `footnotes._mark` also accepts a first piece that is only a mark, on the baseline, when it is a line of its own, the note text starts `footnote_mark_gap` (new setting, 1.0 em) or more right of it, and the page's text above the zone prints the same mark set smaller (`_body_marks`). So "1999 erschien …" is not cut: a year is not a piece of its own and is not printed as a superior mark above. Notes 2/3 and 4/5 split by the same rule.
- **L6 (#459) heading "I".** `_Headings._numeral_one`: a block whose whole text is "I" (or "I.") in the style of a heading found as a Roman numeral alone ("II", "IV.") becomes a heading at that level. A pronoun "I" on a line of its own is body style, so it is never in such a style; a list label is not a block of its own.
- **L7 (#456 criterion 2) German contents.** `_CONTENTS_TITLE` gains "inhalt" and "inhaltsverzeichnis", found by the same rules as "Contents" (title alone on its row, entries ending in a page number at the right edge or after dot leaders). Kehren p4 is not found by this rule: its title shares a framed line with the journal name, and its page numbers stand at the left edge with entries of up to four lines. That page is covered by C1 through the outline. Contents with page numbers at the left edge: follow-up issue #529; a title sharing its row with other words stays out on purpose, since one word at the start of a line is too weak.

Measured with the prototype of L1 to L6 plus P1/P2 (scratch, not pushed; PR A alone gives the same heads, labels and chapters on Kehren, since on main the shifted heads were already in the band and L3 was the blocker): on Kehren, 14 running heads become furniture (all article pages but p5, whose head line also carries "AUS ANLASS DES 100. GEBURTSTAGS VON H.-J. HEYDORN": stays text, a known limit), notes get labels 1 to 5, 2/3 and 4/5 are separate, the script reads "‚Bildung für nachhaltige Entwicklung‘, kurz BNE" and "Fußnote 1." after its sentence, "… Ineinssetzung von Kompetenzentwicklung und realpolitischer …" reads on, chapters "I", "II", "III" exist. On the corpus: every block of the 8 papers identical to main, all 63 snapshots and 1,438 corpus, layout, extraction, textprep-corpus and CLI-golden tests pass. **The shared corpus snapshot `test_app_corpus.ambr` does not move.**

### PR B: lane:extraction, non-critical (#458 extraction part); opened after PR A merges

- **P1** `_Frame` in `extraction/pdf.py`: positions are taken from the crop box's top left corner (`page.get_bbox()`, the box PDFium shows and `get_size()` measures), for characters, baselines, pictures and drawings.
- **P2 text cut off by the crop box is dropped.** An item wholly outside the crop box whose middle lies on the page's own media box (`FPDFPage_GetMediaBox`, only when the page states one) is not printed and is dropped: Kehren p2 is one half of a spread (media 913 pt wide), and the other half (the journal's back cover) would otherwise be read. Text outside the media box is kept as today (a fixture writes 1,000 characters past the page edge; dropping all off-page text would lose text in malformed files).
- **P3 follow-up issue (filed with the PR):** a page with `/Rotate` keeps positions in unrotated space while `width`/`height` are swapped (pre-existing; P1 changes those positions from negative y to the unrotated frame, the text read is unchanged, `turned-page` test passes).
- Corpus: every corpus page has its crop box at the origin (checked all 8 papers), so extraction goldens are unchanged.

### PR C: critical, WAITS FOR APPROVAL (#456 criteria 1, 3, 4)

Front matter named by the outline becomes chapters marked as the publisher's, deselected by default like EPUB publisher pages (#190).

- **C1 layout:** an outline entry whose title is a front-matter name (new setting `front_matter_names`, English and German: Cover, Titelei, Impressum, Imprint, Copyright, Inhalt, Inhaltsverzeichnis, Contents, Table of Contents; compared with `title_key`, trailing punctuation off) starts a level-1 section at the first block of its page when no heading stands on that page. It ends at the next heading or at the first block of the next page any outline entry points to (by page, not outline order), whichever is first. Front matter also adds "Front Cover" to the names. The first such section starts at block 0, so a repository cover sheet before it (Kehren p1) joins it: **the cover sheet is found only through the outline, not by shape** (decided here, criterion 3).
- **C2 core (critical, lane:core + lane:infra):** `Section.publisher: bool = False`, set by C1 only. `Section` is not stored in any versioned file (readalong format and caches hold chapters, not sections; no snapshot holds a `Section`), so no old-reader test is needed; named in the PR.
- **C3 app (`app/chapters.py`, lane:app):** a chapter is the publisher's when its section is (`or` the EPUB tags); blocks after a publisher section's end and before the next section become a chapter named with the document's title, as blocks before the first section already do. On Kehren: Cover (p1 to p2), Impressum, Inhaltsverzeichnis (deselected), then the title chapter (p5: the head line, "Yvonne Kehren", "Bildung und Nachhaltigkeit": criterion 4), "Zur Aktualität …", I, II, III, Literatur, Internetquellen.
- Section starting at a non-heading block: the layout docstring and `test_each_section_starts_at_its_heading` (corpus truth) get the exception "a publisher section from the outline starts at its page's first block".

**D-755 conditions for PR C:** Section.publisher named in the PR body as a core dataclass change, with the reason no old-reader test applies (Section is in no versioned file); the corpus-truth test exception worded in the test and the docstring exactly as above; a test shows a front-matter chapter deselected by default and still choosable; the cover sheet found only through the outline, with a test for a PDF without an outline (nothing becomes front matter); Linux CI and own ci:full green before review.

**Choice (option 1 chosen, D-755):**
1. **C1 + C2 + C3 in one critical PR** (lanes core, layout, app; the app lane thread on #469/#470 does not touch `chapters.py`). Recommended: the role travels in the data, the same rule as EPUB, and the reader can still choose to listen.
2. Layout-only: a new `FurnitureRole.FRONT_MATTER` on every line of those pages. Also a core change, plus a textprep role name (`textprep/furniture.py`, textprep lane busy); the reader cannot choose to hear it, and it differs from EPUB.
3. No core change: layout makes plain sections from the outline names and `app/chapters.py` marks chapters by title. Cheaper, but "the role" is a guess from a title in a second layer.
