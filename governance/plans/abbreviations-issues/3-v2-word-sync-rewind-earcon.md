## Scope
- **Word-level read-along**: read-along format version 2 with per-word times per chunk (`[start, end, start_char, end_char]`). English from the voice's token times (dropped today); German from Kokoro's predicted sound lengths (to be measured). Every word of a replacement maps to its printed span, so "multi-layer perceptron" highlights the printed "MLP". Pairs with read-along on page images (#155).
- **Rewind-to-expand**: the audio job speaks an alternate, with the term in full, of every utterance holding a short-spoken term (at most about 7% of utterances, measured); output writes them into one `<book>.alternates.mp3` listed in `audio.json`. Going back into such an utterance plays the alternate, then continues the chapter MP3. Works only in sci2audio's own player (D-573).
- **Earcon**: an optional short tone where a term was expanded, mixed in by output at the word time.
- **Future idea, maybe v2 (D-574)**: local LLMs, never cloud. Nothing is designed yet, and they make no suggestions to the person's list.
