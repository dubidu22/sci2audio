Feature MVP of the approved abbreviation design: the voice says a term in full where the **paper itself defines it**, and the short form after that.

Design: `plans/abbreviations-architecture.md` in the project files, sections 5 to 9 (approved by Colin on 14 Nov 2026 as D-573; local LLMs are a future idea only, D-574). This issue is its "Feature MVP".

## Scope
- New text step `acronyms` in a new category "Acronyms" (German "Akronyme"), on by default: finds definitions "Full Phrase (ABBR)" and "ABBR (Full Phrase)" (also German hyphenated phrases), every use of a defined term, also uses before the definition (title, abstract).
- At the definition, the bracket is read with a bridge phrase: "Multi-Layer Perceptron, abbreviated as MLP," / German ", kurz MLP,".
- Later uses: one `replace` edit per use with the full phrase. English inflection: plural on the head noun ("ROIs" → "regions of interest"), possessive, hyphen compounds ("fMRI-based"), and the article re-chosen ("an MLP" → "a multi-layer perceptron"). German: only where no ending changes (long form without a leading adjective, not after a genitive article); otherwise the short form is read.
- `choose_expansions` (textprep, called by the app after the chapter choice): counts uses in the order they are heard (`StepRun.read_at` from #213), says the first n in full per scope (audiobook / chapter), marks the rest with a new status `kept-short` (read as printed). A written-out long form counts as a hearing. Defaults: n = 1, scope = chapter, the definition counts as the first full use.
- **An abbreviation the paper does not define is read as printed** (assumed well known; D-573). No built-in list of meanings.
- Folds in #296 (a pronunciation list of acronyms said as words, not meanings) and a homonym guard for defined terms the voice would say as ordinary words (measured: WEIRD, SMART, AIM). Letter pace (#295) learns dotted forms if respelling forces letters.
- `glossary.json` (format "sci2audio-glossary", version 1) in the working folder, written by the script job.
- Report rows for expansions, bridges and kept-short uses (English and German strings).
- Player: read-only glossary panel and a tooltip on hover or tap, reachable by keyboard through the panel.
- Settings: the switch, n (1 to 5), scope (audiobook / chapter). No change to `core`, the chunk key or the read-along file.

## Tests and golden files
- Phase 0 first: measure definition finding against hand-made truth on the corpus; voice readings; a listening sample per bridge phrase in both languages.
- Unit tests per rule in both languages; English and German regression cases first (they fail on main).
- New corpus truth section for definitions (golden, critical) and a corpus test against it.
- Chunk-key test: same settings give the same keys; changing n changes only utterances with that term.
- Hypothesis property: every printed character read once, spans in order, over random documents with definitions.
- Browser test: the highlight stays on the sentence with the printed "MLP" while the phrase plays.
- Snapshots that change: `test_app_corpus.ambr`, `test_app_audio_job.ambr`, `test_cli_golden.ambr`, the regression TOMLs.
