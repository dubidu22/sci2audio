Feature v1.1 of the approved abbreviation design: let the person shape it. Follows the abbreviations MVP issue.

Design: `plans/abbreviations-architecture.md` in the project files, sections 5.3, 8 and 9 (approved by Colin on 14 Nov 2026 as D-573).

## Scope
- **The person's own list of meanings** (short form, meaning, say as), a rule file in the app's config folder, kept **only by the person**: sci2audio ships no list and makes no suggestions (D-573). Terms in it follow the same n-use rules as terms the paper defines; anything in neither is read as printed.
- Per-term overrides per document (`glossary-overrides.json`): meaning, say in full default / always / never, say as voice / letters / word; edited in the glossary panel.
- Export of the glossary as Markdown and CSV, and print to PDF from the glossary page (no PDF library).
- "Text shows: printed text / full terms" in the player.
- More scopes: per paragraph, and every N spoken words (shown with "about X minutes" from the measured characters per second).
- Bridge phrase text, and when the short form is named: at the definition / each time the full term is said / when switching to the short form / never.
- Pause around abbreviations: none / short (commas in the spoken text).

## Tests
List file round trip and refusal of broken entries; overrides round trip; export snapshots; browser test of the "full terms" toggle; settings validation (API 422 cases, English and German strings).
