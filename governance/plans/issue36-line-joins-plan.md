# Issue #36: a space only where the page prints one (plan)

Thread "Join line pieces only where printed", 13 Nov 2026. Critical (changes golden files), so
this plan waited for the coordinator's approval: approved 13 Nov 07:27 UTC, Q1 to Q7 as
recommended (section 9). The numbers
below come from measurements on the 7 corpus papers and from a throwaway prototype that was
deleted afterwards (kept only as a patch in the thread's scratch folder).

## 1. What is wrong today, measured

Stray spaces come from two places (the issue and Colin's comment of 12 Nov 22:55):

1. **Layout joins** the pieces of one printed line with `" "` in four places:
   `paragraphs._paragraph`, `footnotes._note`, `split.read_rows` (formulas, tables, figures,
   formulas across the page) and, by assumption, `split.split_at` / `part_ends` / `kept`, which
   expect exactly one separator before every part. Of 880 piece joins on the corpus, 128 are in
   paragraphs. Every positive join on one baseline is 1 em or more (extraction splits there,
   #27), so all of those are word spaces. The wrong ones are pieces split off at a baseline
   change: a stacked subscript under a superscript ("σ ext OH", "J 1st 0", "n (−1) E", "ρ E").
2. **Extraction keeps every space PDFium generates.** PDFium has no space characters to go on
   in TeX PDFs (13 real space characters in the whole corpus, 34,070 generated ones), and it
   generates a space whenever the baseline changes, however close the letters stand:
   "the i th row" (0.6 pt), "qualia1 ." (0.5 pt), "O(N3 |N |3)". Measured over all generated
   spaces:
   - **Baseline changes** (origin y differs by more than 0.1 em of the smaller letter): 331
     generated spaces stand in white narrower than 0.15 em. They cluster at 0.5 pt (TeX's
     `\scriptspace` after a sub- or superscript, about 0.05 em) and 1.2 pt (the null delimiter
     of a fraction, 0.12 em). Real word spaces at a baseline change measure 0.2 em and more
     (Hardt's "sets. 1" is 5.0 pt, 0.46 em; a checkbox "✓ A" 0.21 em; maths medium spaces
     0.21 em). Only 22 fall between 0.10 and 0.15 em, all inside Liu's and Nowak's maths.
   - **Touching letters** (white of 0 or less): 161 generated spaces, all next to a calligraphic
     maths letter ("|N |", "N ,", "T -"). No real word space in the corpus has touching boxes.
   - **Same baseline, some white**: real word spaces go down to 0.02 em ("of A", the "f" box
     reaches into the space). Geometry cannot second-guess PDFium here, so this plan leaves them.

## 2. Alternatives weighed

| | Approach | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A | Fix the layout joins only | Not enough: "ith", "qualia1." and "|N|" sit inside one extracted line; both `NOT_YET_ONE_BLOCK` entries would stay |
| B | Layout strips stray spaces from line text | Rejected: the block text would no longer hold each line's text at its offset (the text-at-offset invariant, read-along, "keep everything referenceable") |
| C | Extraction records PDFium's "space before" for each line (new `TextLine` field) and layout follows it | Rejected: changes a core dataclass, and PDFium's own choice is exactly what is wrong at scripts |
| D | Extraction stops splitting a line at a stacked script (accepts the jump back) | Rejected for now: changes #27 line splitting everywhere (columns, tables, accents), far wider than this issue |
| **E** | **Extraction drops PDFium's generated space where no white of a space stands; layout joins pieces by the same measure** | **Recommended**. One rule in two stages; no core change; real space characters always kept |

## 3. Recommended design (E)

**Extraction (`extraction/pdf.py`)**. `_chars` reports whether a space was in the file or
generated by PDFium, and each character's baseline (`FPDFText_GetCharOrigin`, turned into the
text direction like the boxes). A *generated* space is left out when:
- the letters on either side touch or overlap (white of 0 or less), or
- the baselines differ by more than `SHIFT` (0.1 em of the smaller letter) and the white is
  narrower than `SPACE` (0.15 em of the larger letter), measured from where the letters of the
  printed row end. A stacked script that extraction splits off into a line of its own ("0"
  under "1st" in J^1st_0) passes on where its row's letters end, so "J1st0[n" gets no space
  before "[".

A space character from the file is always kept. `SPACE` and `SHIFT` are module constants
beside `LINE_GAP` (extraction takes no settings object).

**Layout**. One helper (`geometry.separators`) decides what stands before each piece of a
printed line, used by paragraphs, footnotes and `read_rows`. With `reach` = where the letters
of the row so far end (as `split_cells` already measures):
- white of at least `word_space` (0.15 em of the row's letter size) → `" "`;
- white narrower than that, or a piece that starts back under the end of the row's letters
  (a stacked script) → `""`;
- a piece that starts more than `stack_reach` (2 em) back, under earlier text and so out of
  its place, keeps `" "`, so words never glue ("Physically, me", "pages 321–340.").
Pieces without boxes (EPUB, text) keep `" "`. Both thresholds go into `LayoutSettings`.

`split.split_at`, `part_ends` and `kept` learn that a part may follow with no separator (they
now drop one character before each part, which would cut a letter).

**Truth files**: unchanged. Both `NOT_YET_ONE_BLOCK` entries go (the set becomes empty).

## 4. Prototype result on the corpus

- Layout golden: 123 block lines change, 561 spaces removed, none added (381 in paragraphs,
  116 formulas, 49 figures, 6 captions, 5 footnotes, 4 list items). All 8 footnote-mark and
  inline-maths verbatim sentences pass, including the two in `NOT_YET_ONE_BLOCK`; Hardt's
  "sets. 1" keeps its space.
- Checked against page crops: "5https://…", "1Specifically", "4A PCFG" (footnote marks set
  close to their text), "Biggs 1, Matthew" (JOSS affiliation marks), "language 5." (Arsiwalla
  prints a space before the mark and none after), "J1st0[n(−1)E]", "σextOH", "O(N3|N|3)".
- Spoken script (textprep rules golden): "qualia[1] ." becomes "qualia[1]." (8 such cases in
  Arsiwalla), "P: [7]" becomes "P:[7]".
- Fast suite with the prototype: everything passes except goldens, two paragraph unit tests
  that pin the old `" "` join, and the links corpus tests (below, Q5).

## 5. Design questions (coordinator decides; recommendations given)

- **Q1 Where the fix lives.** Extraction and layout (E) vs layout only (A).
  *Recommend E*: the issue's acceptance criteria and Colin's comment need both.
- **Q2 Threshold.** 0.15 em (below a thin space, 1/6 em) vs 0.10 em (scriptspace only) vs
  0.20 em. *Recommend 0.15 em*: clears scriptspace and null delimiters, keeps every measured
  word space and thin/medium maths space. 0.20 em would cut "✓ A" and medium maths spaces.
- **Q3 Touching letters on one baseline.** Drop a generated space whose letters touch (161
  cases, no real space lost), or leave it to a new issue. *Recommend include*: same bug class
  ("|N |3"), one line of code.
- **Q4 A piece far back.** Keep a space for a piece starting more than 2 em before the reach
  vs always join without one. *Recommend keep the space*: otherwise misplaced pieces glue to
  words ("Physically,me", "pages321–340.").
- **Q5 The links step and "5https://".** With the space gone, `textprep.links` no longer finds
  the URL in Chi's footnote 5 (its start must not follow a letter or digit), so the URL would be
  read aloud; the links corpus tests catch it. *Recommend a minimal fix in this PR*: in a
  FOOTNOTE block that starts with its label, links are searched after the label, plus a
  regression case. Alternative: a separate issue, which leaves a regression on main meanwhile.
- **Q6 Unit tests that pin the old join.** `test_a_footnote_mark_or_subscripts_do_not_cut_a_paragraph`
  and `test_the_gap_after_a_subscript_is_measured_from_the_line_s_end` expect `" "` before a
  mark 1 pt away. *Recommend*: their expected text becomes the printed one ("grammar.1"); the
  second test's 0.2 pt gap between "band off-diagonal" and "terms" is not something extraction
  produces (it splits at 1 em), so that fixture gets a realistic 1 em gap. Nothing is weakened.
- **Q7 Where thresholds live.** Extraction: module constants (`SPACE`, `SHIFT`) like
  `LINE_GAP`. Layout: `LayoutSettings.word_space`, `LayoutSettings.stack_reach`. *Recommend as
  stated*; the two 0.15 values cite each other in comments.

## 6. Tests

- Extraction, made-up PDFs (`tests/unit/pdf_maker.py`): a superscript mark right after a word
  and a full stop after it ("qualia1."), a mark after a word space ("sets. 1"), a subscript
  then a word on the baseline after a word space, touching maths letters; plus a real space
  character at a baseline change stays.
- Layout, made-up pages (issue AC): a footnote mark right after a word, a subscript right
  after its letter, two words split at a wide gap (#27), a stacked sub/superscript, and a piece
  2 em back; each comes out as printed, and every part keeps its item id and exact offset
  (`block.text[start:start+len(line.text)] == line.text`).
- `split` helpers: `split_at`, `part_ends`, `kept` on blocks whose parts join with no
  separator (no letter lost).
- Hypothesis: the existing layout properties (never fails, every item in one block) plus a new
  one: for random rows of pieces, each piece's text sits at its part's offset and a separator
  is "" or " ".
- Corpus: `NOT_YET_ONE_BLOCK` emptied; truth tests unchanged; extraction, layout and textprep
  goldens rebuilt fresh and every change explained in the PR (all are removed spaces).
- Links regression case for "5https://…" (if Q5 as recommended).
- Review checks from memory: text-at-offset invariant on the corpus, nightly Hypothesis
  profile on `test_layout*.py`, mutation of each new rule.
