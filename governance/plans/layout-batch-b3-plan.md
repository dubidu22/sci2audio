## PR 1: #529 + #535, CRITICAL (golden moves), lane:layout

Files: `layout/furniture.py`, `layout/build.py`, `layout/settings.py`, tests.

- **L1 (#529) left-edge entries.** `_contents` tries the right-edge entries as today first; if none follow the title, `_left_entries`: a row of two or more pieces whose first piece is a bare number (1 to 4 digits) at most `contents_reach` em from the text's left edge, its second piece (the entry text) within `contents_reach` em of the first entry's text indent; then up to `contents_rows` (new setting, 4) more rows starting right of the page-number column and within `contents_reach` em of that indent. Page numbers never go down from entry to entry, so a numbered heading set like an entry after the contents ("1 Introduction" after page 30) ends it. Any other row ends it. Title words unchanged (Contents, Table of Contents, Inhalt, Inhaltsverzeichnis). A row whose number and text are one piece is not taken (no gap to measure; out of scope, the German page has two pieces).
- **L2 (#535) page number in the head.** `furniture.head_labels`: for every line found as a running head, each number at its start or end is read as the page number plus an offset (the `_texts` reading of #458). The same head text with the same offset on `repeat_pages` pages or more gives each of those pages that number, as printed, as its label. `build._Layout.label` keeps the page number standing on its own first (#31); the head's number only labels a page that prints none. A number that does not follow the pages ("Heft 55" on every page, "Algorithm 3") gives no label.

## PR 2: #466, CRITICAL, waits for approval (lane:layout, file `extraction/text.py`)

Where: the text reader, not layout. `read_text` already decides a plain-text paragraph (D-344); deciding where an element starts inside it is the same kind of reading, and the tagged reader stays a tag reader (D-405). Done in `layout/tagged.py` instead, an EPUB `<p>` with `<br>` lines would be split too, moving EPUB goldens. Markdown gets list items from markdown-it in the same layer (`extraction/markdown.py`), so plain text then matches it.

- **T1 list items.** Inside a paragraph, a line that opens with "- ", "* " or "• " starts a list item: tags `ul, li` (Markdown's), its wrapped lines `ul, li, br`. A line that opens with "1. " or "1) " (1 to 3 digits) starts one when it is part of a run of two or more such lines in the paragraph whose numbers go up by one in the same style ("1." "2."): one numbered line alone stays text, so "1. Introduction" between blank lines stays a paragraph (its truth comment holds) and a German date or ordinal at a paragraph's start ("3. Oktober 1990 …", "100. Geburtstag …") is never a list item. Markdown would make "1. Introduction" an item; this is the one difference, chosen for the German dates. The marker stays in the text as printed; layout gives the block kind LIST_ITEM (no label in this PR, as Markdown items have none; a follow-up issue if wanted).
- **T2 title lines.** The line after line *k* of a paragraph starts a new paragraph (tag `p`) when line *k* holds at least two letters, is no longer than `TITLE_SHARE` (0.6) of the paragraph's longest line, and either (a) has no lower-case letter ("LISTENING TO PAPERS", "PAPERS HÖREN"), or (b) does not end in `. ! ? : ; , …`, a hyphen or a dash (closing quotes and brackets looked through) and the next line starts with a capital (opening quotes and brackets looked through). Measured: the titles are 0.43 and 0.28 of their paragraphs' longest lines; every non-last line of the samples' prose is 0.86 or more; the poems' lines are 0.64 or more, and each ends in a comma or semicolon or is followed by a line starting lower case. A hard-wrapped all-capitals licence text keeps its lines joined (none is short).
- **Constants** in `extraction/text.py` (`TITLE_SHARE`, the marker patterns), named in its docstring; D-344's description there is extended.
- **Golden moves, each explained:** `test_extraction_text_corpus.ambr` (both samples: title's next line `p` not `p br`; list lines `ul li`), `test_layout_tagged_corpus.ambr` (sample-plain 8 → 10 blocks, beispiel-text 7 → 9: title and subtitle apart, two LIST_ITEM blocks), `test_layout_language.ambr` (the German sample's recorded script: blank lines between title and subtitle and between the two points). `test_app_corpus.ambr` and `test_cli_golden.ambr` do not move.
